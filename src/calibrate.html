Drag the dots

For each place you can recognise, drag its dot (not the label) onto the exact summit, lake or town. Use Show to jump to it and zoom in with the scroll wheel. Mark anything you can't pick out as Can't see.

Line up the join

The phone sweep overlaps itself near north-north-west, and the far mountains don't quite match. Open the aligner, then drag, rotate (slider or [ ] keys) and if needed scale the top picture until the distant mountain ridges line up with the one underneath. Ignore the near ground.

Trace the route

Click along where you walked, starting at the road end and finishing at the top. Click the actual path on the ground; zoom in for the near bits. Points only need to be every few hundred metres on the far spur, closer together near the top.

For a stretch you can't see (behind a hill), tick Hidden, click roughly where it goes, then untick it. Dragging still looks around.
